# Recording the assessed demo

Use `Resolv_Detailed_Presenter_Script.pdf`, included with the release.
It gives one presenter the introduction and assigns one complete scenario to
each of the other three presenters.

| Time | Presenter | What to demonstrate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00-2:00 | Introduction | Sign-in screen, employee and administrator views, and the three scenarios. |
| 2:00-7:15 | Library Wi-Fi | Submit a report with location, show its assignment and notification, then resolve, verify and close. |
| 7:15-12:30 | F102 smartboard | Attach evidence, request and reply to a question, record the repair, verify and close. |
| 12:30-18:00 | Vishwakarma Bhavan power outage | Submit reports from two accounts, review the match, show SLA breach and escalation, then verify restoration and close the primary incident. |

Use one shared demo database. Prepare employee, technician and administrator
browser profiles. Set up Classroom AV and Facilities Power as services.
For the last scenario, give Facilities Power sufficient criticality to preview
P1, keep the primary issue unacknowledged, and rehearse the demo clock jump
against the actual SLA calendar. Verify assignment and case visibility before recording.
